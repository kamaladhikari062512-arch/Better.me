Simple home routine

Start gently and increase gradually. Rest if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual weakness.

  • Squats — 2 sets of 8–12
  • Wall or knee push-ups — 2 sets of 5–10
  • Glute bridges — 2 sets of 10
  • Plank — 2 × 15–25 seconds
  • Easy walk and light stretching

Aim for regular meals, affordable protein sources and adequate sleep. If weakness is persistent or worsening, seek medical advice.
